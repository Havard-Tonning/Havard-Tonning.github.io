Mølla Gjestehus

Mølla Gjestehus is a cozy restaurant by the fjord in Olden, offering guests a relaxed atmosphere and a taste of Norwegian cuisine.

The menu focuses on local products, including fresh fish and flavorful burgers made from locally sourced meat. The concept is simple: honest food that reflects the region and creates a genuine dining experience.

Our bar serves a selection of drinks, including beer from Olden Brewery, bringing a taste of the region into every glass.

On Sundays, Mølla Gjestehus serves a traditional Sunday Roast, creating a warm and social setting for both locals and visitors.

Mølla Gjestehus is a place where guests can slow down and enjoy good food, drinks, and the atmosphere of the fjord.
