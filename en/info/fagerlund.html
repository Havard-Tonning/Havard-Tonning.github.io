Fagerlund 2

Relax with the whole family at this peaceful residence between mountains and fjords in Sunde, by the blue-green Oldevatnet.

Here you have many hiking opportunities if you like hiking in the mountains. Klovane, Kjenuken/Høgenibba and Kattanakken are among many popular summit hikes in the area.

5 min drive to Briksdalen one way and 15 min to Loen the other way. 30 min to Stryn.

The cabin is well-equipped with ready-made bed linen. Towels and cleaning are included in the price.
