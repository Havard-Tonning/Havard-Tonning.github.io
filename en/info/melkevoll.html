Melkevoll Bretun

Melkevoll Bretun Camping can be found at the end of Oldedalen, 20 minutes drive from Olden.

Our unique camping is surrounded by huge mountains, glaciers and waterfalls, and is the starting point to the famous Briksdal glacier.

You can enjoy big private terraces for camping, high standard cabins, and book your space online in advance if you want.

In addition we have sauna, yogaclasses, climbing, café, and is startingpoint for amazing mountain hikes.
