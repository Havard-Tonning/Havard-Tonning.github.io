Briksdalsbre Fjellstove

For generations, Briksdalsbre Fjellstove has been a meeting place for everyone who is hiking in Briksdalen.

In Fjellstova you can enjoy delicious dishes after your hike to the glacier. The restaurant has seating for more than 550 guests. Our extensive souvenir shop is located right next to Fjellstova. Here you can shop tax free - and of course we accept all credit cards.

Filled with powerful impressions and with fresh air in your lungs, it is nice to spend the night in Briksdal.

You will feel at home here!
